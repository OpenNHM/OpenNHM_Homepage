## About OpenNHM

**OpenNHM (Open Natural Hazard Modeling)** is an initiative that extends beyond avalanche
simulation to encompass a comprehensive suite of natural hazard modeling tools.
It provides a platform for natural hazard modeling tools, enabling researchers,
practitioners, and institutions to collaborate on the development of new approaches
and tools. Building on AvaFrame's experience, OpenNHM aims to create an ecosystem
for multi-hazard simulation and analysis.
